WHEN YOUR MIND IS LOUD

A 90-second reset

USE ANYTIME
01Exhale slowly

Take a comfortable breath in, then let the exhale be a little longer. Repeat gently.

02Name the state

Say: “I'm noticing resistance / anxiety / distraction.” A feeling is information, not an order.

03Make it smaller

Ask: “What is the smallest useful action I can finish in five minutes?” Do only that.

These are practical self-help tools, not a diagnosis or a replacement for professional care. If distress is persistent, severe, or affecting daily life, reaching out to a qualified mental-health professional is a strong next step.
